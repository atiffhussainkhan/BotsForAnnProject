**Version:** 4.0
**Effective Date:** 27 October 2026
**Applies To:** All Instagram Reels, Feed Posts, Stories, Carousels, and TikTok Videos published by `@annreveals`.

## 1. Golden Rules for `@annreveals`

1. **Buying a bottle yourself does NOT cancel out an Affiliate Link or TikTok Shop link.**
   - If you bought a perfume with your own money **and** you include an affiliate link, discount code, or TikTok Shop product link, UK law (ASA & CMA) still classifies the post as an **Ad**.
   - In that situation, start your caption with **`Ad |`** and switch **ON** the platform’s commercial toggle—you can still truthfully say: *"Ad | I bought this bottle with my own money (contains affiliate link)."*
2. **Never write `"Not an ad"` if there is any link, code, gift, or commission.**
   - Writing `"Not an ad"` when you have an affiliate link in your bio/caption or a discount code is a high-risk UK ASA & CMA violation.
3. **When a review is 100% self-bought with ZERO links, codes, or gifts:**
   - Do **NOT** turn on TikTok’s "Disclose video content" toggle or Instagram’s "Paid partnership" label (because turning them on tells the platform your video is a paid ad).
   - Upload your receipt to the **Verified Purchases Vault** first. The app will automatically scrub your card details and home address and keep a secure cryptographic proof in case TikTok or Instagram ever flags the video by mistake.
4. **Always use `Ad` upfront—never rely on `#gifted`, `#pr`, or `#aff` alone.**
   - Whenever a disclosure is required in the UK, put **`Ad |`** right at the very beginning of the caption before people have to tap *"…more"*.
5. **Keep claims specific to the bottle in your hand.**
   - Say *"I bought this bottle myself and these are my personal impressions."*
   - Never make sweeping account-wide promises like *"I never accept gifts or sponsorships on this page"* so you never trap your future content.
6. **Confirm `"Experian"` vs `"Empyrean"` receipts once in the Vault.**
   - If a receipt or voice note says `"Experian"`, the app will ask you to confirm with one tap that you meant **Empyrean Parfums** (and will never confuse it with the credit agency `experian.com`).
7. **Keep receipts private.**
   - Never post screenshots of your invoices, order numbers, or bank statements publicly in comments or stories. If a platform moderates a post, use the **Appeal Packet Builder** to export a safely redacted proof bundle.
